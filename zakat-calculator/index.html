New to this? Which standard should I use?

Nisab — the minimum wealth threshold before zakat is due — is traditionally set using either gold (85g) or silver (612.36g) as the reference metal. In today's market these give very different dollar thresholds, because gold is worth far more per gram than silver.

Silver gives a lower threshold. More people whose wealth has grown end up liable for zakat, which better serves zakat's purpose of transferring wealth to those in need — this is why most contemporary scholars and zakat organisations recommend it as the default.

Gold gives a higher threshold and is the more classical primary reference in some traditional texts. Some scholars prefer it, particularly when a person's wealth is mostly held in gold itself.

Both are recognised, valid positions — this isn't a case of one being wrong. This calculator defaults to silver; switch the dropdown above if you'd rather use gold, or ask your own trusted scholar if you're unsure which applies to you.
