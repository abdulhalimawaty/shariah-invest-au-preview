Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site has no accounts, no logins, no cookies and no advertising trackers. Nothing you type into the calculators ever leaves your own browser.

What this site does not do

There are no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter capture, no comment system and no advertising or social-media tracking pixels. This site sets no cookies of its own, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

The calculators run entirely in your browser

The Zakat, Growth and Tax calculators are plain JavaScript running on your own device. The figures you enter — your cash, gold, shares, salary, HECS-HELP debt, and everything else — are never transmitted anywhere. They aren't sent to a server, aren't stored, and aren't visible to us. Close the tab and they're gone. We could not see them even if we wanted to.

Analytics

We use privacy-focused, aggregate analytics to see which pages people find useful — for example, how many visits a guide received in a month. This is deliberately chosen to be cookie-free and to collect no personal information: no cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles, no attempt to identify individual visitors, and no data sold or shared with anyone. We can see that a page was viewed; we cannot see who viewed it.

External links

This site links out to fund providers, regulators, AAOIFI, ASIC, the ATO and similar organisations. Once you follow one of those links you're on their website under their privacy policy, not this one.

Your rights

Because the site holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, export or delete on request. If that ever changes, this page changes with it — and the date below will move.
