Project your portfolio's growth

Regular contributions, compounded over time, at a return rate you choose. This is a math tool for your own assumptions — not a projection tied to any interest-bearing product, and not a promise of return.

New to investing? How to use this calculator

Starting amount is what you'd invest today. Monthly contribution is what you'd add every month after that — this is usually the part that matters most, more than the return rate. Time horizon is how many years you'll leave it invested; the longer it is, the more compounding does the work for you instead of your own contributions.

Assumed annual return is the one you have to guess, so here's real context: over the 30 years to June 2026, Australian shares averaged about 9.8% a year, and international shares performed similarly (Vanguard's 2026 Index Chart). That's a historical average across a broad market index, not a promise — and a Shariah-screened portfolio excludes whole sectors (banks, conventional REITs, most miners with heavy debt), so it can perform quite differently. If you're unsure what to enter, a more conservative 6–8% is a reasonable starting point rather than assuming the historical average will repeat.

Whatever you enter, this stays a "what if" projection — real markets go up and down year to year, this chart just doesn't show that noise.
