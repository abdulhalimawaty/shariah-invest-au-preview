Is Charter Hall Long WALE REIT (CLW) Shariah-compliant?

Excluded
Excluded on business activity: REIT — conventional debt structure. No financial ratio screen applies here — a company in this category is excluded regardless of its balance sheet. See the full business-activity criteria.

Why no ratios are shown

The business-activity screen runs before any financial test. Because Charter Hall Long WALE REIT's core business falls inside an excluded category, the debt, cash and receivables ratios are never reached — a strong balance sheet cannot offset a non-compliant core activity, so publishing those figures here would imply a borderline call that doesn't exist.

This is a screening verdict on the listed entity's primary business, not a judgment about the company or the people in it. If you think the classification is wrong — for instance where a diversified group's excluded activity is a small share of revenue — the methodology page sets out exactly where the lines are drawn and why.
